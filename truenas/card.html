Box card

The card that goes in each box. Fill in its serial and setup code from the factory script's printout, then print both sides.

Print with ⌘P or Ctrl P: two A6 pages, front then back. On card stock, print double-sided and flip on the long edge, at 100% scale.

Getting started

  1. Connect the box to your router with the network cable, then plug in the power.
  2. Give it about three minutes to start.
  3. Scan the front of this card, or open obsidian.local, and follow the steps on screen.

Have these ready

  • A Plex account. Free at plex.tv.
  • Your VPN account. Mullvad, Proton VPN, NordVPN and others. Downloads only ever run through it.
  • A TMDB key. Free at themoviedb.org, for titles and posters.
